Cookie Garden

For this project, I designed a virtual relationship garden where each plant represents a meaningful connection in the user’s life. The plants are abstract forms that grow and change based on interaction data—such as check-ins, time spent together, or shared moments. “Watering” symbolizes intentional effort, like reaching out or reflecting on a memory, which helps the plant grow. Over time, consistent care makes the plant more vibrant, while neglect causes it to fade. Each relationship has its own record, allowing users to plant new connections and build a living ecosystem that visually demonstrates how relationships thrive through attention and care.
Softwares used: Html, CSS, Javascript
