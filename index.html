Hi, I'm Tarini Singhania, currently a New York based designer majoring in Communication Design at Parsons School of Design. My interest lies with motion graphics, branding, web (UI/UX) design & creative art direction.

Originally from India, I bring a unique perspective to my work, blending diverse cultural insights with cutting-edge design principles. Passionate about creating meaningful visual experiences that communicate effectively across various platforms. To do so, I explore with humor, culture & colors to ensure that there’s never a dull moment in the work I create.
