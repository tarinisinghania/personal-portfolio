TIPS[I]

Problem Statement

How might we allow new drinkers to easily know how much to drink that will help avoid blackout nights and hangovers?

Value proposition

Tipsy is the app that allows new drinkers to determine their alcohol limits by caculating recommended comsumption judging by health and daily lifestyle.

Key Features

  1. Recommended amount of drinks
  2. Linked to voop and apple whatch to track sleep and fitness
  3. Menstrual cycle tracker
  4. Quiz on day before nightout
  5. Hangover tracker + cure
